## 1. Attendance and matters arising

Present: test kitchen lead, two development bakers, purchasing representative.

Actions from the previous meeting were reviewed. The new proving cabinet has
been installed and is holding temperature. The order for replacement baking
stones has been placed.

## 2. Equipment

Oven 3 was reallocated to the hot cross bun launch for six weeks. During that
time it was not available for other trial bakes.

Ovens 1 and 2 remained in use for daily production tests. Neither can hold the
large stones used for sourdough trial bakes.

The mixer in bay 2 was serviced. No fault was found.

## 3. Trial bakes

Twelve trial bakes were completed in the quarter. Results are recorded on the
trial bake sheets and are not repeated here.

Sourdough trial bakes resumed when oven 3 was returned.

## 4. Any other business

The test kitchen will close for deep cleaning on the last Friday of each
month. The next meeting will be held in the first week of the second quarter.
