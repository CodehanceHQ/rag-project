## 1. Purpose

This procedure governs the classification and approval of changes affecting
material specification, and sets out the record to be retained against each
class of change. It applies to all product families and to all functions
raising a change against a controlled specification.

The procedure does not itself confer authority. Authority to approve a change
of a given class is conferred by the delegation of authority in force on the
date the change is raised, and is held as described in that instrument.

## 2. Change classes

Changes affecting material specification are classified on the basis of the
characteristic affected and the effect of the change on qualified performance.
Classification is determined at the point the change is raised and is recorded
against the change request.

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Class A | Alters a characteristic affecting a safety-critical interface |
| Class B | Alters a qualified characteristic, no safety-critical interface |
| Class C | Alters a non-qualified characteristic |
| Class D | Documentation-only; no characteristic altered |

A safety-critical interface is an interface identified as such in the approved
technical file for the product family. Identification is made at design
approval and is not revisited at the point a change is raised.

Where the classification is not evident, the raising function refers the
change to the Quality Management System function for determination before the
change proceeds. A change may not be progressed under an assumed class.

Class D covers changes correcting a drawing note, a reference, a document
number or a descriptive entry, where no characteristic of the product is
altered by the change. A change that alters a characteristic is not Class D
however small the alteration.

## 3. Authority levels

For changes affecting a safety-critical interface, the following provisions
apply in addition to the general provisions of this section.

Authority to approve changes is conferred in levels. The level required for a
given change follows from the class determined under section 2. Levels are
held by role, and the role holding each level is set out in the delegation of
authority in force at the relevant date. This procedure does not name role
holders, and a copy of this procedure is not evidence of who held a level on
any given date.

Where authority at the required level is not available within the raising
function, the change is referred upward. Authority may not be exercised by a
role holding a lower level, and may not be delegated downward other than by an
amendment to the delegation of authority itself. A change approved at an
insufficient level is not validly approved and is treated as outstanding until
approval at the correct level is recorded.

Where the delegation of authority is amended between the date a change is
raised and the date it is approved, the instrument in force on the date the
change was raised governs. This provision exists to prevent a change acquiring
or losing an approval requirement through the passage of time alone.

Level 3 approval is required for a change of Class A.

| Class | Level required |
| --- | --- |
| Class A | Level 3 |
| Class B | Level 2 |
| Class C | Level 2 |
| Class D | Level 1 |

Level 1 approval is required for a documentation-only change of Class D, and
may be exercised within the raising function.

## 4. Records

The record retained against a change is the change request itself, recording
the change, the date raised, and the procedure revision under which it was
processed. The classification determined under section 2 and the approval
recorded against it are held by the Quality Management System function and are
not reproduced on the change request.

| Record | Held by | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change request | Raising function | Life of product plus ten years |
| Classification determination | Quality Management System | Life of product plus ten years |
| Approval record | Quality Management System | Life of product plus ten years |
| Delegation of authority in force | Corporate Governance | Permanent |

Requests for the approval record against a specific change should be made to
the Quality Management System function, quoting the change request reference.
