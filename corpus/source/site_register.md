## 1. Sites

Sunrise Bakery operates three sites. Each site is listed with its address and
the manager responsible for it.

| Site | Address | Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High Street bakery | 14 High Street | Site manager, High Street |
| Riverside bakery | Unit 3, Riverside Yard | Site manager, Riverside |
| Station Road bakery | 2 Station Road | Site manager, Station Road |

A site is added to this register when it opens and removed when it closes.

## 2. Functions

Each site carries out the functions listed against it. A function not listed
against a site is not carried out there.

| Site | Functions |
| --- | --- |
| High Street bakery | Baking and retail sale |
| Riverside bakery | Dough preparation and shaping. It does not bake. |
| Station Road bakery | Baking, retail sale and wholesale dispatch |

Shaped dough from the Riverside bakery is sent to the other two sites for
baking.

## 3. Approved sources by site

Sources approved to deliver to each site are listed by supplier code. Supplier
names and addresses are held in the supplier register and are not repeated
here.

| Site | Approved supplier codes |
| --- | --- |
| High Street bakery | SUP-0311, SUP-0407 |
| Riverside bakery | SUP-0207, SUP-0407 |
| Station Road bakery | SUP-0207, SUP-0311 |

A delivery from a source not approved for the receiving site is refused at
the door.
