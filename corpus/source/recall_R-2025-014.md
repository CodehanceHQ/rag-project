## 1. Scope and affected product

This notice records the field action raised under reference R-2025-014 and
sets out the product affected, the population in scope, and the actions
required of holders of affected stock.

The action applies to the valve assembly BV-12 only. The assembly is
identified on the product label and on the packing documentation by its part
number. Holders should confirm the part number before applying any action
described in this notice, as adjacent assemblies in the same series are not in
scope and have not been assessed under this action.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Reference | R-2025-014 |
| Raised | 2025-02-06 |
| Classification | Field safety action |
| Product affected | BV-12 valve assembly |
| Status | Open |

The population in scope is defined by build date and by the batch identifiers
listed in section 3. Assemblies outside those identifiers are not affected and
require no action. Units already returned under separate arrangements are
excluded from this action and are tracked separately.

This notice supersedes Issue 1, which was withdrawn following a revision to
the batch identifiers in section 3. No other part of the notice was changed.

## 2. Defect description

The condition arises at the seal interface between the valve body and the
housing. Under sustained thermal cycling at the upper end of the declared
operating range, the interface may lose sealing contact, permitting seepage
past the seal face.

The condition develops progressively. Early indications are a small deposit
at the interface and a measurable reduction in retained pressure across a
hold test. Complete loss of sealing contact has not been observed in the
field and is not expected within the assessed service interval, but the
condition is progressive and does not self-correct.

| Characteristic | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Seal interface, valve body to housing |
| Onset | Progressive, under sustained thermal cycling |
| Indication | Deposit at interface; reduced retained pressure |
| Detection | Hold test at service interval |

Assessment of the condition and of the corrective design activity is recorded
under separate engineering references and is not reproduced in this notice.
Holders requiring the engineering assessment should request it through the
usual channel.

## 3. Affected batches

The following batch identifiers are in scope. Identifiers are printed on the
assembly label and repeated on the packing note.

| Batch | Build period | Units in scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5102 | 2024-10 | 340 |
| 5118 | 2024-11 | 412 |
| 5133 | 2024-12 | 388 |
| 5147 | 2025-01 | 295 |

Batches outside this list are not in scope. Where a batch identifier is
illegible, the assembly should be treated as in scope until the build period
can be confirmed from the packing documentation.

Units held as uncommitted stock should be quarantined and reported on the
return schedule described in section 4. Units in service should be assessed at
the next planned maintenance opportunity and reported whether or not the
condition is present, as the population figures depend on complete returns.

## 4. Containment actions

Holders of affected stock are required to quarantine uncommitted units
immediately and to report holdings against the reference above.

| Action | Applies to | Required by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarantine | Uncommitted stock | On receipt of this notice |
| Report holdings | All holders | 14 days from receipt |
| Assess in service | Units in service | Next planned maintenance |
| Return affected units | Units confirmed affected | On assessment |

Replacement assemblies are available against the same part number from
current production. Current production is outside the batch identifiers listed
in section 3 and is not affected by this action.

Correspondence relating to this action should quote the reference above. The
action remains open until the return position is reconciled and the closure
record is raised.
