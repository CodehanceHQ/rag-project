## 1. Scope

This instruction covers the assembly of the BV-13 valve assembly from received
sub-components to the point of final inspection. It applies at all sites
holding the assembly in scope of work.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Instruction | WI-BV13 |
| Revision | Revision B |
| Issued | 2025-06 |
| Applies to | BV-13 valve assembly |

Operators shall work to the current revision only. Superseded revisions are
withdrawn from the line and retained by Manufacturing Engineering.

## 2. Tooling

| Item | Specification | Calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Torque wrench | Range covering the specified value | 6 months |
| Assembly fixture | Fixture FX-BV13-01 | Annual verification |
| Seal insertion tool | Tool ST-16 | Visual check each shift |
| Depth gauge | Per drawing tolerance | 6 months |

The torque wrench shall be selected such that the specified value falls within
the middle of the instrument range.

## 3. Sub-assembly

Components shall be drawn from released stock only and shall carry the batch
identifier on the accompanying documentation. Clean all mating faces before
assembly.

| Step | Operation | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Clean mating faces | Visual |
| 3.2 | Inspect seal seating groove | Visual and gauge |
| 3.3 | Fit seal using insertion tool ST-16 | Visual |
| 3.4 | Verify seal seating depth | Depth gauge |

The seal shall be fitted using the insertion tool ST-16. The insertion tool
for this assembly is not interchangeable with tooling for other assemblies in
the series; confirm the tool number before fitting.

## 4. Housing assembly

The housing is secured to the valve body with the M10 housing bolts supplied
in the assembly kit. Confirm that the kit is complete and that the fastener
dimension matches this instruction, as kits for other assemblies in the series
carry a different fastener and are not interchangeable.

Present the housing squarely and seat it by hand until the flange faces meet.
Engage all fasteners by hand before applying torque. Fasteners shall be fitted
dry; do not apply lubricant, sealant or thread locking compound.

Tighten in a diagonal sequence to 55 Nm. Recheck after 30 minutes.

| Step | Operation | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | Confirm kit complete and fastener dimension | Build record |
| 4.2 | Seat housing, confirm flange contact | Visual |
| 4.3 | Engage all fasteners by hand | Visual |
| 4.4 | Apply specified torque in sequence | Build record |
| 4.5 | Recheck at the stated interval | Build record |

Where the recheck finds a fastener below the specified value, the sequence
shall be reapplied in full and a further recheck performed.

## 5. Inspection

| Check | Method | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flange contact | Visual and feeler | No measurable gap |
| Fastener torque record | Build record | All entries present |
| Recheck record | Build record | Present, at stated interval |
| External condition | Visual | Free of damage and residue |

An assembly failing any check shall be quarantined and reported. Conforming
assemblies shall be labelled and passed to packing.
