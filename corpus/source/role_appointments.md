## 1. Appointments by role

This record lists appointments to roles carrying formal responsibility, with
the period each appointment was held. It is maintained by Human Resources and
reissued annually or on a material change to the structure.

Roles are listed under the title in use at the time of the appointment. Where
a role was renamed or discontinued at the functional reorganisation, the entry
records the title in use during the period held.
