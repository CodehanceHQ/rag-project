## 1. Introduction and revision history

This bill of materials lists the line items making up each assembly in the BV
series, with the part number, quantity and approved source against each item.
It is issued by Engineering and reissued at each edition.

This edition supersedes edition 3. Line items altered at this edition are marked in the revision history below.

| Edition | Issued | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edition 4 | 2024-01 | Fastener kit, BV-13 |
| Edition 3 | 2023-01 | General reissue |
| Edition 2 | 2022-01 | General reissue |

Sources listed against a line item are those approved for the item at this
edition. Approval is held per site and is recorded in the supplier register
maintained by Procurement; this document does not record site approval.

Where more than one source is approved for a line item, any approved source
may be drawn. Where a single source is listed, no alternative is approved.
