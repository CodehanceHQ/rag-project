## 1. Batch identification

This report records the assessment of batch 4471 at incoming inspection and
the disposition applied to the batch.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Batch | 4471 |
| Material | Elastomer seal, housing interface |
| Received | 2024-08-14 |
| Quantity received | 900 |
| Quantity sampled | 32 |

The batch was sampled in accordance with the incoming inspection plan for the
material category. Sampling was performed on receipt and before the material
was made available to production.

## 2. Test results

Sampled items were assessed against the material specification for hardness,
compression set and dimensional conformity. Dimensional conformity and
compression set were within specification across the sample.

Hardness measurements fell below the lower specification limit in 11 of the
32 items sampled. The batch was recorded as **seal hardness out of tolerance**
and the sample extended in accordance with the inspection plan. Extended
sampling confirmed the original finding across the batch.

| Characteristic | Specification | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardness | Within declared band | Below lower limit, 11 of 32 |
| Compression set | Within declared band | Conforming |
| Dimensional | Within drawing tolerance | Conforming |
| Extended sample | 64 items | Finding confirmed |

No further testing was performed. The material was not released to production
at any point and no assemblies were built from this batch.

## 3. Disposition

The batch was rejected in full and quarantined pending return to the supplier.
A supplier notification was raised on the date below and the material was
collected against the return note recorded here.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Disposition | Reject in full |
| Quarantined | 2024-08-16 |
| Supplier notified | 2024-08-16 |
| Return note | RN-2024-0812 |

Replacement material was requested against the original order. Receipt of the
replacement is recorded separately on the goods receipt register.
