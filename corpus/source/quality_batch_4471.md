## 1. Batch details

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Batch | 4471 |
| Product | Classic Sourdough (SD-12) |
| Mixed | 2025-01-14 |
| Inspected by | Quality |
| Result | Rejected |

Batch 4471 was rejected at dough inspection. The finding was dough hydration
out of tolerance.

## 2. Finding

The dough was wetter than the recipe allows. It would not hold its shape after
the first fold and spread on the bench.

The water meter on the mixer was checked and found to be reading low. The
meter was replaced the same day.

## 3. Disposition

The whole batch was written off. No loaves from batch 4471 were baked or sold.

A replacement batch was mixed after the meter was replaced and passed
inspection.
