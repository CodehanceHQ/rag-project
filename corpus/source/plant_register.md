## 1. Sites and codes

This register lists the manufacturing sites operated by the group, the site
code used in internal documentation, and the function each site performs. The
register is maintained by Manufacturing Operations and is reissued when a site
is opened, closed, or reassigned.

| Site code | Site | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plant X | Bamberg | Assembly and test |
| Plant Y | Kassel | Machining and sub-assembly |
| Plant Z | Ingolstadt | Assembly, test and rework |

Site codes are used throughout internal documentation in preference to
location names. Where a document refers to a site by location only, the code
in this table governs.

No other manufacturing sites are operated by the group. Warehousing and
distribution locations are not manufacturing sites and are listed in the
logistics register, which is maintained separately.

## 2. Plant X (Bamberg)

Plant X performs final assembly and test. The site operates two assembly
lines and a test cell, and holds approval for the assembly families listed in
its scope of work.

| Attribute | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Site code | Plant X |
| Location | Bamberg |
| Function | Assembly and test |
| Lines | 2 assembly, 1 test cell |
| Scope | Valve and sealing assemblies |

Incoming material at Plant X is received against the approved supplier list
for the site, recorded in section 4. Material from a supplier not on that list
may not be drawn to the line without a concession raised by Quality.

The site operates its own incoming inspection function. Nonconformities
raised at incoming inspection are recorded against the site and reported
monthly. Rework performed at Plant X is recorded under the site code in the
monthly rework return.

## 3. Other sites

Plant Y at Kassel performs machining and sub-assembly and does not carry out
final assembly. Output from Plant Y is transferred to Plant X or Plant Z for
assembly.

Plant Z at Ingolstadt performs assembly and test and additionally operates the
group rework cell. Rework returned from the field or from other sites is
routed to Plant Z, which is reflected in the site's monthly rework figures.

| Site | Lines | Rework cell | Incoming inspection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plant Y (Kassel) | 3 machining | No | Yes |
| Plant Z (Ingolstadt) | 2 assembly | Yes, group | Yes |

Transfers between sites are recorded on the internal movement note and do not
constitute a supply event for the purposes of the approved supplier list.

## 4. Approved supplier lists by site

Each site draws material only from the suppliers approved for that site. A
supplier approved at one site is not thereby approved at another; approval is
granted per site following audit.

Approved sources are recorded here by supplier code. Codes resolve to
companies in the supplier register maintained by Procurement; this register
does not reproduce company names, which change on reorganisation while the
code does not.

| Site | Approved source codes |
| --- | --- |
| Plant X (Bamberg) | SUP-0311, SUP-0407 |
| Plant Y (Kassel) | SUP-0142, SUP-0407 |
| Plant Z (Ingolstadt) | SUP-0142, SUP-0311 |

A code appearing against one site does not imply approval at another.
Approval is granted per site following audit, and a supplier approved at one
site has not thereby been audited against another site's scope of work.

Changes to an approved source list require a completed site audit and are
recorded in the supplier register.
