Notification lodged with the competent authority in respect of the field
safety action recorded under reference R-2025-014.

| Field | Entry |

The following undertaking was given at notification and is recorded here.

| Undertaking | Interval | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow-up report to the competent authority | 90 days from acknowledgement | 2025-06-09 |

returned units, and the effectiveness of the containment applied. The report

this notification is held by Regulatory Affairs under the reference above.
