## 1. Scope

This instruction covers the making of the Country Sourdough, product code
SD-13, from mixed dough to the point of final inspection. It applies at every
site that bakes the product.

Bakers shall work to the current revision only.

## 2. Equipment

| Item | Specification | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rack oven | Rotating rack, with steam | Daily |
| Loaf tins | 900 g, lidded | Each use |
| Probe thermometer | Reads to one degree | Weekly against reference |

Loaf tins shall be greased lightly before each use. Tins that are dented or
have a damaged coating shall be taken out of use.

## 3. Shaping and proving

Dough arrives divided and rested. Each piece is shaped into a log and placed
seam side down in a greased tin.

Prove until the dough reaches the rim of the tin. A loaf that rises above the
rim before baking has proved too far.

## 4. Baking

The loaves are baked in the rack oven, in their tins, on the rotating rack.
Confirm before starting that the rack turns freely and that every shelf is
seated.

Load the tins with a gap between them so that air can move around each one.

Bake with steam for the first 5 minutes. Set it to 210 °C. Check the core
temperature after 45 minutes.

Turn the loaves out of their tins as soon as they leave the oven. A loaf left
in its tin sweats and the crust softens.

## 5. Inspection

Final inspection is carried out when the loaves have cooled on the rack for
one hour. Loaves that fail inspection are recorded on the waste sheet and are
not sold.
