## 1. Site services

This guide describes the services Facilities provides at each site: cleaning,
pest control, waste collection and repairs to the building.

A fault with the building is reported on the facilities form and is seen by
the next working day. A fault with baking equipment is not a facilities matter
and is reported to the shift supervisor.

Cleaning contractors attend every site after closing. Pest control visits are
monthly.

## 2. High Street site quality overview

The High Street bakery is the oldest of the company's sites and the busiest
for retail sale. Its quality arrangements are the same as those at every site.

Quality at the High Street bakery is the responsibility of the site manager.
Any quality problem at the High Street bakery, including a recurring quality
problem, is reported through the site manager to the Quality function, which
keeps the reports.

Facilities does not hold quality reports for the High Street bakery or for any
site. This guide records no quality findings.

The High Street bakery is cleaned nightly and has a deep clean on the first
Sunday of each month.

## 3. Waste and recycling

Food waste is collected daily. Cardboard and paper are collected on Mondays
and Thursdays. Flour sacks are returned to the mill that supplied them.

## 4. Contacts

The facilities desk is staffed from 07:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. Out of hours,
urgent building faults go to the duty manager.
