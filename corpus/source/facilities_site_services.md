## 1. Site services

This handbook sets out the services provided by Facilities across the group's
manufacturing sites, the standard of service applying to each, and the route
for requesting service outside the standard provision.

| Service | Standard provision | Request route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cleaning, production areas | Daily, end of second shift | Standing |
| Cleaning, office areas | Daily | Standing |
| Waste removal, general | Twice weekly | Standing |
| Waste removal, controlled | Weekly, scheduled | Booking |
| Grounds maintenance | Fortnightly, seasonal | Standing |
| Pest control | Monthly inspection | Standing |
| Compressed air, plant | Continuous | Standing |
| Process water | Continuous | Standing |
| Heating and ventilation | Per site profile | Standing |

Services listed as standing are provided without request. Services listed as
booking require a request through the facilities booking system, raised at
least five working days before the service is required.

Requests outside the standard provision are assessed on receipt and confirmed
or declined within two working days. Where a request is declined, the reason
is given and an alternative provision proposed where one exists.

Utilities provision is monitored continuously and reported monthly. Reporting
covers consumption against the site profile and any interruption to supply,
with the duration of each interruption and the service affected.

| Utility | Monitoring | Reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electricity | Continuous, metered | Monthly |
| Compressed air | Continuous, metered | Monthly |
| Process water | Continuous, metered | Monthly |
| Gas | Continuous, metered | Monthly |

Interruptions to utilities provision are logged and reported irrespective of
duration. The log is held by Facilities and is available to site management on
request.

Access to plant rooms is restricted to Facilities personnel and to contractors
holding a current permit. Permits are issued under the arrangement in
section 3.

## 2. Bamberg site quality overview

This section describes the facilities provision supporting quality activity at
the Bamberg site and the standards to which that provision is maintained. It
describes facilities, not product.

The site operates a dedicated quality area comprising an incoming inspection
bay, a measurement room and a document store. The measurement room is
environmentally controlled and monitored continuously, as measurement
equipment held there requires stable ambient conditions to retain calibration
between scheduled intervals.

| Area | Provision | Environmental control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incoming inspection bay | 140 square metres, bench and rack | Ambient |
| Measurement room | 45 square metres | Controlled, monitored |
| Document store | 30 square metres, racked | Controlled humidity |
| Quality office | 60 square metres | Ambient |

Environmental conditions in the measurement room are logged continuously and
reported monthly to site management. The service standard for the room is
stated below and has been met in every reporting period since the current
revision of this handbook was issued.

| Parameter | Service standard | Reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | Within stated band | Monthly |
| Relative humidity | Within stated band | Monthly |
| Ambient particulates | Within stated band | Quarterly |
| Lighting level at bench | Within stated band | Annual |

The quality areas are cleaned to the enhanced standard applying to controlled
areas, which is a separate provision from the general production cleaning
standard set out in section 1. Cleaning of the measurement room is performed
by Facilities personnel trained in the enhanced standard and is recorded
against each attendance.

Facilities provision for quality activity at the site is reviewed annually
with site management. The last review confirmed the provision as adequate for
the activity held at the site, with no change requested.

The quality areas hold the document store for the site. Storage provision,
racking and retrieval arrangements are a facilities matter; the content of
stored records and any activity recorded in them are outside the scope of this
handbook and of Facilities generally.

## 3. Contractor access

Contractors requiring access to site shall hold a current permit. Permits are
issued by Facilities on receipt of a completed application and the required
documentation.

| Permit class | Covers | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 1 | General access, escorted | Single visit |
| Class 2 | General access, unescorted | 12 months |
| Class 3 | Plant rooms and roof access | 6 months |
| Class 4 | Hot work and confined space | Per task |

Applications are assessed against the documentation required for the permit
class. Incomplete applications are returned and are not held pending.

| Documentation | Class 1 | Class 2 | Class 3 | Class 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insurance certificate | Required | Required | Required | Required |
| Method statement | Not required | Not required | Required | Required |
| Competence evidence | Not required | Required | Required | Required |
| Task risk assessment | Not required | Not required | Not required | Required |

Permit holders shall report to reception on each attendance and shall sign in
and out. A permit does not confer access outside the areas stated on it.

Permits may be withdrawn by Facilities at any time. Withdrawal takes effect
immediately and is notified to the contractor and to the requesting function.
