## 1. Report

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Report | NCR-2025-031 |
| Site | High Street bakery |
| Raised | 2025-02-18 |
| Raised by | Quality |
| Finding | Underweight loaves |

At the weekly weight check, 14 of 60 large loaves sampled at the High Street
bakery were under the minimum baked weight.

## 2. Action

The loaves sampled were taken off sale. The bench scale at the dividing table
was checked against the reference weight and found to read correctly.

The report was closed on 2025-02-25.
