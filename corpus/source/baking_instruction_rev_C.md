## 1. Scope

This instruction covers the making of the Classic Sourdough, product code
SD-12, from mixed dough to the point of final inspection. It applies at every
site that bakes the product.

| Field | Entry |

| Supersedes | Revision B |

This revision corrects the equipment list. No other provision of the
instruction is altered by this revision.

Bakers shall work to the current revision only. Superseded revisions are
withdrawn from the bakery floor and kept by Production for record purposes.
Where a superseded revision is found at a workstation it shall be returned to
Production and replaced.

## 2. Equipment

Equipment required for the product is listed below. Equipment shall be clean
and in working order at the start of each shift. Equipment found faulty shall
not be used and shall be reported to the shift supervisor.

| Item | Specification | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deck oven | Stone deck | Daily |
| Proving baskets | Cane, 1 kg size | Each use |
| Scoring blade | Single edge, in holder | Each shift |
| Bench scale | Reads to one gram | Weekly against reference |

The bench scale shall be wiped down before and after each use. A scale that
has not been checked against the reference in the past week shall not be used
for this product.

Proving baskets shall be brushed out after each use and left to dry. Baskets
that are damp or show mould shall be taken out of use.

Scoring blades shall be changed at the start of each shift. A blunt blade
drags the dough and spoils the shape of the loaf.

## 3. Shaping and proving

Dough arrives divided and rested. Each piece is shaped into a round, placed
seam side up in a floured proving basket and covered.

| Step | Operation | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Shape each piece into a tight round | Visual |
| 3.2 | Place seam side up in a floured basket | Visual |
| 3.3 | Cover and prove until risen by half | Poke test |
| 3.4 | Record the prove on the build sheet | Build sheet |

A loaf is ready when a floured finger leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
A dent that springs back at once means the loaf needs longer. A dent that does
not spring back means the loaf has proved too far and shall be reshaped.

Proving is complete when every loaf on the rack has passed the poke test.
Loaves shall not go forward to baking with a failed check.

## 4. Baking

The loaves are baked in the deck oven, directly on the stone. Confirm before
starting that the stone is clean and that no crumbs or burnt flour are left
from the previous bake; burnt flour marks the base of the loaf and gives a
bitter taste.

Turn each loaf out of its basket onto the loading peel, seam side down. Handle
the loaf gently so that it keeps the gas built up during proving. A loaf that
collapses when turned out has proved too far and shall be set aside for
reshaping rather than baked.

Score each loaf with a single cut across the top before loading. Scoring lets
the loaf open in a controlled way as it rises in the heat. A loaf that is not
scored will tear at the side, and a torn loaf fails final inspection.

Loaves shall be loaded with space between them. Do not let loaves touch on the
stone and do not load more than the rack sheet allows. Loaves that touch bake
unevenly, stay pale where they meet, and leave soft patches in the crust that
shorten the life of the product.

Set it to 230 °C. Bake until the crust is a deep brown.

The bake shall be recorded on the build sheet against the batch number. A
batch released without a recorded bake is not conforming and shall be held
until the record has been completed.

| Step | Operation | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | Confirm stone clean | Build sheet |
| 4.2 | Turn out and score each loaf | Visual |
| 4.3 | Load with space between loaves | Visual |
| 4.4 | Bake as specified | Build sheet |

## 5. Inspection

Final inspection is carried out when the loaves have cooled on the rack for
one hour.

| Check | Method | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crust colour | Visual against the colour card | Within the card range |
| Shape | Visual | Round, opened along the score |
| Base | Tap | Sounds hollow |
| Weight | Scale | Within tolerance for the product |

Loaves that fail inspection are recorded on the waste sheet and are not sold.
The build sheet is signed by the baker and kept under the records procedure.
