## 1. Trials

Six trial bakes of the SD-12 loaf were run in oven 3 in March 2025. Each trial
baked twelve loaves and recorded the core temperature of every loaf on leaving
the oven.

| Trial | Steam | Lowest core reading | Loaves gummy at the centre |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | None | 88 | 5 of 12 |
| 2 | None | 90 | 4 of 12 |
| 3 | None | 87 | 6 of 12 |
| 4 | First 10 minutes | 96 | 0 of 12 |
| 5 | First 10 minutes | 97 | 0 of 12 |
| 6 | First 10 minutes | 96 | 0 of 12 |

Core readings are in degrees Celsius.

## 2. Observations

In trials 1 to 3 the crust set within the first few minutes and the loaves
stopped rising early. Loaves with a core reading under 94 were gummy at the
centre when cut.

In trials 4 to 6 the crust stayed soft for longer and the loaves rose fully.
Every loaf read 96 or above at the core and none was gummy.

The oven setting was the same in all six trials.

## 3. Records

Trial bake sheets are kept by the Test Kitchen for two years.
