## 1. Complaint

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Complaint | SC-2025-009 |
| Raised | 2025-05-12 |
| Raised by | Purchasing |
| Raised against | Dough divider service contractor |
| Site | High Street bakery |

Following the service visit to the dough divider at the High Street bakery on
2025-05-06, the divider cut pieces lighter than its setting. Loaves baked from
those pieces were underweight.

## 2. Response requested

The contractor is asked to return, reset the divider and confirm the setting
in writing.

The contractor attended on 2025-05-15 and reset the divider. The complaint was
closed on 2025-05-16.
