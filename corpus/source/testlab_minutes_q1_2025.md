## 1. Attendance

Quarterly coordination meeting of the test laboratory, held to review rig

Apologies were received from the Engineering liaison for the second half of
the meeting. Minutes were circulated for comment and are recorded as agreed.

## 2. Rig utilisation

Utilisation across the four rigs was reviewed for the quarter. Rigs 1 and 2
operated at capacity throughout. Rig 4 was available but under-utilised, with
two open windows in February that were not taken up.

| Rig | Utilisation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rig 1 | 96% | At capacity |
| Rig 2 | 94% | At capacity |
| Rig 3 | 88% | See allocation below |
| Rig 4 | 41% | Two open windows in February |

Allocation for the coming period was agreed, with rig 3 reallocated to the injector programme for six weeks with effect from the start of the period, following the request recorded at the previous meeting. Existing bookings on
rig 3 were offered rig 4 where the fixture permits.

Two bookings could not be transferred, as the fixture is not interchangeable
between rigs 3 and 4. Those bookings were returned to the requesting function
to rebook once rig 3 is released.

The chair noted that rig 4 utilisation remains the standing item and that the
fixture interchangeability question should be resolved before the next
allocation round.
