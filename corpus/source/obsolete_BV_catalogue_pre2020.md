## 1. BV series overview

The BV series comprises valve assemblies for industrial service across the
nominal bore range listed in this catalogue. Assemblies are supplied complete
and tested, with the seal set fitted.

This edition supersedes edition 3 and all earlier editions. Specifications are
those current at the date of issue and are subject to change without notice.
Confirm current specification with Sales Engineering before ordering.

## 2. Specifications

Specifications are stated by assembly group. Values are nominal and apply at
the reference condition stated in the general notes.

| Group | Body material | Seal set | Connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BV-10 to BV-15 | Cast body | Standard | Threaded |
| BV-16 to BV-22 | Cast body | Standard | Threaded or flanged |
| BV-23 to BV-28 | Fabricated body | Heavy duty | Flanged |
| BV-29 to BV-33 | Fabricated body | Heavy duty | Flanged |

Pressure ratings are stated on the assembly nameplate and on the test
certificate supplied with each unit. Ratings are not reproduced in this
catalogue.

## 3. Ordering

Orders should quote the order code in full, the quantity required, and the
delivery address. Orders quoting a model designation without an order code
will be referred back.

| Order element | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order code | Yes | In full, as listed |
| Quantity | Yes | Minimum order applies |
| Delivery address | Yes | Site and contact |
| Required date | No | Best endeavours if omitted |

Minimum order quantities apply by assembly group and are confirmed at
quotation. Lead times are quoted at order acknowledgement and are not stated
in this catalogue.

<!--page-break-->

| Group | Minimum order | Packaging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BV-10 to BV-15 | 10 | Cartoned |
| BV-16 to BV-22 | 5 | Cartoned |
| BV-23 to BV-28 | 2 | Crated |
| BV-29 to BV-33 | 1 | Crated |

Returns are accepted only against a returns authorisation issued by Sales
Engineering. Unauthorised returns are refused at the receiving point.

Enquiries should be directed to Sales Engineering quoting this edition.
