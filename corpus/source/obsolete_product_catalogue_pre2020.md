## 1. Breads

The catalogue lists the breads baked for wholesale customers, with a short
description of each.

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Classic Sourdough | Round white sourdough with an open crumb and a crisp crust |
| Country Sourdough | Tin-baked wholemeal sourdough with a soft crust |
| Farmhouse white | Soft white tin loaf for sandwiches |
| Granary bloomer | Malted bloomer with a seeded top |
| French stick | Long white stick, baked twice daily |

Sourdough loaves are made over two days and should be ordered two days ahead.

## 3. Ordering

Orders are taken by telephone before 14:00 for delivery the next morning.
Standing orders can be changed with one day's notice.
