## 1. Scope and affected product

This report records product withdrawal W-2025-014. The product withdrawn is
the Classic Sourdough, product code SD-12.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Withdrawal | W-2025-014 |
| Product withdrawn | Classic Sourdough (SD-12) |
| Raised | 2025-02-10 |
| Raised by | Food Safety |
| Type | Withdrawal from sale |

The withdrawal applies to loaves of the product baked within the dates given
in section 3. No other product in the range is withdrawn under this report.

Loaves outside the affected batches are not withdrawn and may remain on sale.

## 2. Reason for withdrawal

Small pieces of blue plastic were found in two loaves during routine slicing
checks. The pieces match the blade edge of a dough scraper used at the
dividing stage.

The plastic is hard and could injure a customer if swallowed. The product is
withdrawn as a precaution while the affected batches are identified.

No injury has been reported. No complaint had been received from a customer
at the date of this report.

## 3. Affected batches

The affected batches are those divided on the scraper identified in section 2
between the last recorded scraper inspection and the date the damage was
found.

| Batch | Baked | Loaves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4502 | 2025-02-06 | 240 |
| 4503 | 2025-02-07 | 240 |
| 4505 | 2025-02-08 | 180 |
| 4506 | 2025-02-09 | 236 |

Batch numbers are printed on the closure tag of each loaf.

## 4. Containment actions

Loaves from the affected batches are removed from sale and held for return.
Stock already sold is covered by a notice at the point of sale.

All dough scrapers of the same type are inspected and replaced where the
blade edge is worn. Scraper inspection is added to the start-of-shift checks.

Returned stock is counted and recorded on the stock return log. Notification
to the food safety authority is made separately.
