## 1. Scope

This instruction covers the assembly of the BV-12 valve assembly from received
sub-components to the point of final inspection.

| Field | Entry |

This revision is superseded. It is retained for record purposes only and shall

return it to Manufacturing Engineering.

## 2. Tooling

| Item | Specification | Calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Torque wrench | Range covering the specified value | 6 months |
| Assembly fixture | Fixture FX-BV12-02 | Annual verification |
| Seal insertion tool | Tool ST-14 | Visual check each shift |
| Depth gauge | Per drawing tolerance | 6 months |

Tooling shall be within calibration and shall carry a current calibration
label. Calibration status shall be confirmed at the start of each shift and
recorded on the shift log.

## 3. Sub-assembly

Components shall be drawn from released stock only. Clean all mating faces
before assembly; faces shall be free of machining residue, handling marks and
protective compound.

| Step | Operation | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Clean mating faces | Visual |
| 3.2 | Inspect seal seating groove | Visual and gauge |
| 3.3 | Fit seal using insertion tool ST-14 | Visual |
| 3.4 | Verify seal seating depth | Depth gauge |
| 3.5 | Fit internal components per drawing | Visual |

The seal shall be fitted using the insertion tool. Verify seating depth after
fitting. A seal seated outside the drawing tolerance shall be removed and
discarded.

## 4. Housing assembly

The housing is secured to the valve body with the M8 housing bolts supplied in
the assembly kit. Confirm that the kit is complete and that the bolts carry
the correct finish.

Present the housing squarely and seat it by hand until the flange faces meet.
Engage all fasteners by hand before applying torque. Fasteners shall be fitted
dry.

Tighten the fasteners to 40 Nm.

| Step | Operation | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | Confirm kit complete | Build record |
| 4.2 | Seat housing, confirm flange contact | Visual |
| 4.3 | Engage all fasteners by hand | Visual |
| 4.4 | Apply specified torque | Build record |

Where a fastener is found below the specified value at inspection, the
assembly shall be quarantined and reported.

## 5. Inspection

| Check | Method | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flange contact | Visual and feeler | No measurable gap |
| Fastener torque record | Build record | All entries present |
| Seal seating | Build record | Within drawing tolerance |
| External condition | Visual | Free of damage and residue |

An assembly failing any check shall be quarantined and reported. Conforming
assemblies shall be labelled and passed to packing.
