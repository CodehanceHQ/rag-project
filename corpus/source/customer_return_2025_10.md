## 1. Return details

Assessment of a customer return received against the reference below. The
assembly was built at Plant X (Bamberg).

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Return | CR-RET-2025-233 |
| Received | 2025-10-07 |
| Build site | Plant X (Bamberg) |
| Reported condition | Seepage at seal interface |
| Units returned | 4 |

The customer reported seepage observed at the seal interface during routine
inspection. The units were returned complete and unmodified.

## 2. Analysis

The seal was removed from each unit and assessed against the material
specification.

| Unit | Hardness | Dimensional | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Below lower limit | Conforming | Seal hardness below specification |
| 2 | Below lower limit | Conforming | Seal hardness below specification |
| 3 | Within band | Conforming | No material nonconformity |
| 4 | Below lower limit | Conforming | Seal hardness below specification |

Seal hardness below specification was recorded against three of the four
units assessed. The fourth unit returned a conforming result and no material
nonconformity was recorded against it.

Replacement units were despatched to the customer against the original order
reference. The assessed seals were retained.
