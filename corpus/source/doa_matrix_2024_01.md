## 1. Effective date and scope

This instrument sets out the levels of authority conferred on roles within the
group, and the limits applying to each level. It takes effect on 2024-01 and
governs approvals made on and after that date.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument | DOA-2024-01 |
| Effective | 2024-01 |
| Issued by | Corporate Governance |
| Scope | All functions, all product families |

Authority is conferred on a role, not on an individual. Where a role is
vacant, authority conferred on that role is exercised by the role to which it
reports until the appointment is made. The holder of a role at any date is
recorded in the appointments record maintained by Human Resources.

This instrument confers authority. It does not determine which level is
required for a given decision; that follows from the procedure governing the
decision.

## 2. Authority levels 1 and 2

Level 1 is conferred on functional management. It covers decisions taken
wholly within a function, where no characteristic of a product is altered and
no commitment is made outside the function.

| Level | Conferred on | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Functional management | Decisions within a function |
| Level 2 | Senior functional management | Decisions affecting qualified characteristics |

Level 2 is conferred on senior functional management. It covers decisions
affecting a qualified characteristic where no safety-critical interface is
affected.

Authority at either level may be exercised only within the scope of the role
on which it is conferred. A role may not exercise authority in respect of a
product family outside its scope of responsibility.

## 3. Authority level 3

Level 3 is the highest level conferred by this instrument. It is held by the
role set out below.

| Level | Role holding the authority |
| --- | --- |
| Level 3 | Director of Engineering Quality |

Level 3 covers decisions affecting a safety-critical interface, and any
decision expressly reserved to Level 3 by the procedure governing it.

Level 3 authority may not be delegated. Where the role is vacant, decisions
requiring Level 3 are held pending appointment and are not exercised at a
lower level.

The identity of the holder of the Director of Engineering Quality role at any
date is recorded in the appointments record. This instrument names the role
and does not name the holder.
