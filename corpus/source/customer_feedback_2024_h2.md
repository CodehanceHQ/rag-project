## 1. Summary

This summary covers feedback received from July to December 2024 across every
outlet. It counts comments by subject and quotes a sample of each.

| Subject | Comments |
| --- | --- |
| Crumb gummy or wet in the centre | 61 |
| Crust too dark | 9 |
| Loaf sold out before noon | 22 |
| Bag tore on the way home | 7 |

## 2. Gummy centre

Sixty-one customers said the centre of their sourdough loaf was gummy, wet or
doughy. Most of these comments were about the Classic Sourdough.

"The crust was lovely but the middle was like raw dough."

"I had to toast every slice to make it edible."

"Sticky in the centre two weeks running."

Comments on this subject rose month by month from September.

## 3. Other subjects

Comments on dark crust, early sell-out and torn bags were passed to the sites
concerned. Counts for each are in the table in section 1.
