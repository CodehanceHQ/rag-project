## 1. Change description

This change request records a revision to the seal material specified for the
valve assembly identified in section 2. The elastomer grade previously carried
against the seal line item is withdrawn and replaced with FKM-70.

The revision was raised by the Engineering Change Board following a review of
the sealing performance requirement at the housing interface. The replacement
grade offers improved thermal and chemical resistance across the declared
operating range, and is qualified for continuous service at the interface
temperatures recorded in the assembly specification.

The replacement grade is to be reflected in the next issue of the bill of
materials for the affected series. No change is made to the seal geometry, the
housing interface dimensions, or the fastener specification. Tooling is
unaffected.

Downstream qualification activity arising from this change is managed
separately and is not recorded here.

## 2. Affected assemblies

This change applies to the following assembly only.

| Assembly | Line item | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BV-12 | Seal, housing interface | Material grade revised to FKM-70 |

No other assembly in the series is affected by this change request. Assemblies
sharing the housing interface geometry were reviewed and excluded, as the seal
line item is specified separately against each assembly and the revision does
not propagate.

Stock of the withdrawn grade held against BV-12 is to be depleted before the
replacement grade is drawn. Existing finished assemblies are unaffected and no
retrospective action is required against units already built.
