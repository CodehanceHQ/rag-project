## 1. Requirement

An allergen retest is required following the ingredient change. The product
now uses Heritage Stoneground flour, which is milled on a line that also
handles other grains.

The product may not be labelled or sold beyond its present outlets until the
retest has been passed.

## 2. First test

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Classic Sourdough (SD-12) |
| Sampled | 2025-03-04 |
| Laboratory | External, accredited |
| Result | Inconclusive |

The first test did not give a clear result. One of three samples showed a
trace reading at the limit of detection.

## 3. Retest

A full retest on six samples is booked with the external laboratory for
2026-04-14.

This certificate forms part of the file for withdrawal W-2025-014 and is
reissued when the retest result is received.
