## 1. Scope

This instruction covers the assembly of the BV-12 valve assembly from received
sub-components to the point of final inspection. It applies at all sites
holding the assembly in scope of work.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Instruction | WI-BV12 |
| Revision | Revision D |
| Issued | 2025-06 |
| Supersedes | Revision C |
| Applies to | BV-12 valve assembly |

This revision is issued under CR-91. The revision introduces a defined
tightening sequence at the housing interface and a recheck requirement. No
other provision of the instruction is altered by this revision.

Operators shall work to the current revision only. Superseded revisions are
withdrawn from the line and retained by Manufacturing Engineering for record
purposes. Where a superseded revision is found at the workstation it shall be
returned to Manufacturing Engineering and replaced.

## 2. Tooling

Tooling required for the assembly is listed below. Tooling shall be within
calibration and shall carry a current calibration label. Tooling outside
calibration shall not be used and shall be withdrawn to the calibration
cabinet.

| Item | Specification | Calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Torque wrench | Range covering the specified value | 6 months |
| Assembly fixture | Fixture FX-BV12-02 | Annual verification |
| Seal insertion tool | Tool ST-14 | Visual check each shift |
| Depth gauge | Per drawing tolerance | 6 months |

The torque wrench shall be selected such that the specified value falls
within the middle of the instrument range. A wrench used near the limit of
its range shall not be used for this assembly.

Calibration status shall be confirmed at the start of each shift and recorded
on the shift log. Where a tool is found outside calibration during a shift,
assemblies built since the previous confirmed check shall be quarantined
pending assessment.

Fixtures shall be cleaned at the end of each shift and inspected for wear at
the interval stated in the fixture maintenance schedule. Fixture wear affects
seating and shall be reported to Manufacturing Engineering rather than
corrected at the workstation.

## 3. Sub-assembly

The sub-assembly stage prepares the valve body and the internal components
for housing assembly. Components shall be drawn from released stock only and
shall carry the batch identifier on the accompanying documentation.

Clean all mating faces before assembly. Faces shall be free of machining
residue, handling marks and protective compound. Use only the cleaning agent
specified in the materials list; substitution is not permitted.

| Step | Operation | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Clean mating faces | Visual |
| 3.2 | Inspect seal seating groove | Visual and gauge |
| 3.3 | Fit seal using insertion tool ST-14 | Visual |
| 3.4 | Verify seal seating depth | Depth gauge |
| 3.5 | Fit internal components per drawing | Visual |

The seal shall be fitted using the insertion tool. Fitting by hand or with
any other tool is not permitted, as the seal may be stretched beyond its
recovery limit without visible indication.

Verify seating depth after fitting. A seal seated outside the drawing
tolerance shall be removed and discarded; a removed seal shall not be
refitted. Record the check on the build record.

Where the seating groove shows damage, the valve body shall be withdrawn and
reported. Damage to the groove cannot be corrected at the workstation.

Sub-assembly is complete when all checks in the table above are recorded
against the build record. The assembly shall not proceed to housing assembly
with an outstanding check.

## 4. Housing assembly

The housing is secured to the valve body with the M8 housing bolts supplied in
the assembly kit. Confirm before starting that the kit is complete and that
the bolts carry the correct finish; bolts of the correct dimension but
incorrect finish are supplied against other assemblies in the series and are
not interchangeable.

Present the housing to the valve body squarely, taking care not to displace
the seal fitted at the previous stage. Seat the housing by hand until the
flange faces meet. A gap at the flange face indicates that the seal has been
displaced or that debris is present at the interface; in either case the
housing shall be removed and the interface reinspected before proceeding.

Engage all fasteners by hand before applying any torque. Hand engagement
confirms thread alignment and prevents cross-threading, which cannot reliably
be detected once torque has been applied. A fastener that does not engage
freely by hand shall not be forced; withdraw it, inspect the thread, and
replace the fastener if the thread shows damage.

Fasteners shall be fitted dry. Do not apply lubricant, sealant or thread
locking compound at this interface unless expressly directed by a concession
raised against the specific build. Lubricant alters the relationship between
applied torque and achieved clamp load, and a lubricated fastener tightened to
the specified value will be over-tightened at the joint.

Tighten in a diagonal sequence. Set it to 40 Nm. Recheck after 30 minutes.

The recheck is required because the seal relaxes after initial compression and
the clamp load falls. The recheck shall be recorded on the build record
against the assembly serial. An assembly released without a recorded recheck
is not conforming and shall be returned for the recheck to be performed.

| Step | Operation | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | Confirm kit complete and finish correct | Build record |
| 4.2 | Seat housing, confirm flange contact | Visual |
| 4.3 | Engage all fasteners by hand | Visual |
| 4.4 | Apply specified torque in sequence | Build record |
| 4.5 | Recheck at the stated interval | Build record |

Where the recheck finds a fastener below the specified value, the sequence
shall be reapplied in full and a further recheck performed at the stated
interval. A fastener requiring a third application shall be withdrawn and the
assembly reported to Manufacturing Engineering.

## 5. Inspection

Final inspection confirms that the assembly conforms to the drawing and that
all checks required by this instruction have been recorded.

| Check | Method | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flange contact | Visual and feeler | No measurable gap |
| Fastener torque record | Build record | All entries present |
| Recheck record | Build record | Present, at stated interval |
| Seal seating | Build record | Within drawing tolerance |
| External condition | Visual | Free of damage and residue |

An assembly failing any check shall be quarantined and reported. Rework shall
be performed only under a rework instruction raised for the specific
condition; rework at the workstation is not permitted.

Conforming assemblies shall be labelled with the assembly serial and the
build record reference and passed to packing. The build record shall be
retained for the period stated in the records procedure.
