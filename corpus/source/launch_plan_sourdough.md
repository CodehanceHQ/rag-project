## 1. Classic Sourdough launch plan, phase two

Phase two takes the Classic Sourdough from sale in the company's own outlets
to sale through supermarket bakery counters.

| Milestone | Planned date |
| --- | --- |
| Supermarket listing agreed | 2025-01-20 |
| Packaging artwork signed off | 2025-02-03 |
| First supermarket delivery | 2025-03-03 |
| Second supermarket group | 2025-04-07 |
| Full range of stores | 2025-05-05 |

## 2. Schedule of dates

The schedule below gives the revised dates for the same milestones.

| Milestone | Revised date |
| --- | --- |
| Supermarket listing agreed | 2025-01-20 |
| Packaging artwork signed off | 2025-02-03 |
| First supermarket delivery | 2025-06-09 |
| Second supermarket group | 2025-07-14 |
| Full range of stores | 2025-08-11 |

Dates are confirmed with each supermarket two weeks ahead.
