## 1. Approval levels

This matrix states which role holds each approval level. It applies from its
effective date.

| Approval level | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Shift Supervisor |
| Level 2 | Bakery Manager |
| Level 3 | Head of Production |

A level is held by the role, not by a named person. The person appointed to
each role, and the dates of the appointment, are recorded in the role
appointments record.

## 2. Use

A change is approved by the holder of the level its procedure requires, under
the matrix in force on the date the change is raised.
