## 1. Complaint

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Record | CC-2025-044 |
| Received | 2025-10-09 |
| Received by | Customer Service |
| Bought at | High Street bakery |
| Subject | Loaf smaller than expected |

A café that buys twenty large loaves a day from the High Street bakery
reported that the loaves delivered on 2025-10-08 were smaller than usual. The
café weighed six and found each under the weight printed on the tag.

## 2. Resolution

The café was refunded for the delivery and sent replacement loaves the next
morning. The record was closed on 2025-10-10.
