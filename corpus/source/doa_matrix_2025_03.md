## 1. Effective date and scope

This instrument sets out the levels of authority conferred on roles within the
group, and the limits applying to each level. It takes effect on 2025-03 and
governs approvals made on and after that date.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument | DOA-2025-03 |
| Effective | 2025-03 |
| Issued by | Corporate Governance |
| Scope | All functions, all product families |

The instrument is issued following the functional reorganisation, which
altered the roles on which authority is conferred. Roles named here reflect
the structure in place from the effective date.

Authority is conferred on a role, not on an individual. The holder of a role
at any date is recorded in the appointments record maintained by Human
Resources.

## 2. Authority levels 1 and 2

Level 1 is conferred on functional management and covers decisions taken
wholly within a function, where no characteristic of a product is altered.

| Level | Conferred on | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Functional management | Decisions within a function |
| Level 2 | Senior functional management | Decisions affecting qualified characteristics |

Level 2 is conferred on senior functional management and covers decisions
affecting a qualified characteristic where no safety-critical interface is
affected.

The reorganisation did not alter the scope of Level 1 or Level 2. Roles
carrying these levels were renamed in several functions without change to the
authority conferred.

## 3. Authority level 3

Level 3 is the highest level conferred by this instrument. It is held by the
role set out below.

| Level | Role holding the authority |
| --- | --- |
| Level 3 | Head of Product Integrity |

Level 3 covers decisions affecting a safety-critical interface, and any
decision expressly reserved to Level 3 by the procedure governing it.

The Head of Product Integrity role was established at the reorganisation and
carries the authority previously conferred elsewhere in the structure. Level 3
authority may not be delegated.

The identity of the holder of the Head of Product Integrity role at any date
is recorded in the appointments record. This instrument names the role and
does not name the holder.
