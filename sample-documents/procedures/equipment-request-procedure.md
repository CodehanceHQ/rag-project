# Equipment Request and Replacement Procedure

Procedure ID: IT-ASSET-031  
Owner: IT Operations  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 20 February 2026

## Standard equipment

New employees receive a standard laptop, charger, headset, and one external monitor when the hiring ticket is approved. Engineering roles may receive a higher-memory laptop when the manager provides a workload justification.

The normal laptop replacement cycle is four years. Replacement is based on the device's service date, condition, security support, and role requirements. Reaching four years makes a device eligible for review; it does not guarantee an automatic replacement.

## Request and approval levels

Equipment is requested through the IT Service Portal using form IT-42.

- Items costing up to EUR 150 require manager approval.
- Items above EUR 150 and up to EUR 1,000 require manager and cost-centre-owner approval.
- Items above EUR 1,000 require manager, cost-centre-owner, and IT Operations approval.
- Any non-standard laptop requires an IT architecture and security review regardless of price.

Contractors cannot approve equipment purchases, even when they lead a project. An employee who owns the relevant cost centre must provide the approval.

## Home-office equipment

The Remote and Hybrid Work Policy provides a one-time EUR 350 allowance for approved hybrid employees. Purchases under that allowance remain company property unless People Operations states otherwise in writing. Requests beyond the allowance follow the normal approval levels in this procedure.

## Loss, theft, and damage

A lost or stolen device must be reported under the Information Security Policy immediately. Filing an equipment replacement request does not satisfy the security-reporting requirement. After Security records the incident, IT Operations may issue a temporary device.

Accidental damage should be reported through the IT Service Portal. Employees must not arrange third-party repairs because they may expose company information or invalidate device management controls.

## Return and disposal

Equipment must be returned within five working days after employment ends unless People Operations confirms another date. IT Operations records the return, securely erases storage, and sends retired equipment to an approved recycling partner. Employees must not sell, donate, or discard company equipment.
