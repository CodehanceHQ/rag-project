# Company Glossary

Document ID: OPS-GLOSSARY-003  
Owner: Business Operations  
Status: Current

## Terms

**Anchor day:** A day on which a hybrid team is normally expected to work together from its assigned office.

**Business day:** Monday through Friday excluding public holidays at the employee's primary work location.

**Calendar day:** Any day of the week, including weekends and public holidays.

**Cost centre owner:** The employee accountable for approving expenditure against a departmental budget. A contractor cannot be a cost centre owner.

**Company-managed device:** Hardware enrolled in Northstar device management with required encryption, updates, screen-locking, and endpoint-protection controls.

**Effective date:** The date on which a policy, procedure, rate, or amendment begins to control. When two instructions conflict, staff should identify the authoritative document and most recent applicable effective date rather than assuming the newest uploaded file is correct.

**Hybrid role:** A role approved for a recurring combination of office and remote work. A hybrid arrangement does not change the employee's employing country.

**Policy owner:** The function responsible for maintaining and interpreting a policy. Only the policy owner or an explicitly authorized officer may publish a controlling amendment.

**Pro rata:** A proportional calculation based on working pattern or the portion of a year for which an entitlement applies.

**Restricted data:** The highest Northstar information classification, covering material such as authentication secrets, production customer datasets, and private encryption keys.

**Rolling 12-month period:** The 12 months immediately preceding and including the date being assessed, rather than a fixed calendar year.

**Working day:** A day on which the employee is normally scheduled to work. This differs from a business day for employees with non-standard schedules.
