# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-WORK-014  
Owner: People Operations  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 15 February 2026

## Standard working pattern

Employees assigned to a hybrid role may work remotely for up to three days per working week. Their manager and team agree which days are most useful for in-person collaboration. Tuesdays are the default team collaboration day for the Madrid office, but a department head may set a different anchor day.

Remote work is a working-location arrangement, not additional leave. Employees must remain available and productive during their agreed schedule.

## Core collaboration hours

All employees working on Central European time must normally be available between 10:00 and 15:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Meetings outside core hours should be limited and agreed in advance. Flexible start and finish times remain available where team coverage is maintained.

## Working temporarily from another country

An employee may request to work outside their employing country for up to 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period. The request must be submitted at least 30 calendar days before travel and approved by the employee's manager, People Operations, and Security.

Approval is not automatic. Tax, immigration, customer-contract, data-residency, insurance, and time-zone constraints may prevent the arrangement. Work from a country rated Critical by Security is prohibited. Personal travel days and annual leave do not count as remote-working days.

## Workspace and equipment

Employees must use company-managed devices for Confidential or Restricted information. Public wireless networks may only be used with the company VPN. Screens must not be visible to unauthorized people, and printed business information must be securely destroyed.

Northstar provides a one-time home-office allowance of EUR 350 after a hybrid arrangement is approved. The allowance may be used for a monitor, keyboard, pointing device, task lighting, or ergonomic chair. It may not be used for internet subscriptions, rent, decoration, or personal consumer electronics.

## Security incidents

A lost or stolen device, suspected phishing message, accidental disclosure, or unauthorized account access must be reported through the security hotline immediately. The Information Security Policy and Security Incident Response Procedure define the required reporting route and response times.

## Review and withdrawal

Managers review hybrid arrangements every six months. An arrangement may be changed when performance, role responsibilities, security, customer needs, or team coverage require it. A change should normally be discussed at least 14 calendar days in advance.
