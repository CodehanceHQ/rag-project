# Information Security Policy

Policy ID: SEC-104  
Owner: Information Security  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 10 January 2026

## Purpose

This policy establishes minimum security requirements for everyone who accesses Northstar Analytics systems or information, including employees, contractors, interns, and approved third parties.

## Accounts and authentication

Each person must use an individually assigned account. Password sharing is prohibited. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, source-control, cloud, finance, and production systems. Password-manager-generated credentials should be used wherever the system supports them.

A request to bypass multi-factor authentication must be treated as suspicious even when it appears to come from a senior employee. Security will never ask a user to disclose a password or one-time authentication code.

## Devices

Confidential or Restricted information may only be accessed from a company-managed device unless Security has approved a documented exception. Full-disk encryption, automatic security updates, screen locking, and endpoint protection must remain enabled.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the security hotline immediately and no later than 15 minutes after discovery. The reporter should provide the device type, asset tag if known, last known location, approximate time of loss, and whether Restricted information may have been accessible.

## Phishing and suspicious activity

Suspected phishing must be reported using the email client's Report Phishing action. If a person clicked a suspicious link, entered credentials, approved an unexpected authentication prompt, or opened an attachment, they must also call the security hotline immediately. They should not delete the message because Security may need its headers and attachments.

## Information handling

Information must be handled according to the Data Classification and Retention Policy. Restricted data must not be placed in consumer file-sharing tools, personal email, public AI services, or unapproved collaboration systems. Access must be limited to people with a business need.

## Incident reporting

The security hotline is available at extension 7711 and `security-urgent@northstar.example`. Reports involving active account compromise, stolen devices, malware, or accidental public disclosure are handled under the Security Incident Response Procedure.

Good-faith reporting will not result in disciplinary action. Delaying a report to investigate the issue personally can increase harm and is prohibited.
