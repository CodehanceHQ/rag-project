# Annual Leave Policy

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-2026-01  
Owner: People Operations  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 1 January 2026  
Review date: 1 October 2026

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains annual leave for Northstar Analytics employees based in Spain. It applies to permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors should refer to the terms of their service agreement.

## Annual entitlement

A full-time employee receives 25 working days of paid annual leave in each calendar year, excluding public holidays. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement on a pro rata basis. Employees who join or leave during the year receive a pro rata entitlement calculated from their employment dates.

Annual leave is recorded in working days. Weekends and days on which an employee is not normally scheduled to work do not reduce the balance.

## Requesting leave

Employees submit requests in the People Portal. Requests for one or two working days should normally be submitted at least five working days in advance. Requests for three or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least 15 calendar days in advance.

A manager must approve the request before travel is booked. Managers consider customer commitments, minimum team coverage, and the order in which requests were received. Approval should not be unreasonably withheld.

No more than 10 consecutive working days may normally be taken at once. The People Operations Director may approve a longer period for exceptional circumstances.

## Carryover

Under the original terms of this policy, up to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by 31 March or they expire. Statutory exceptions apply where leave could not be taken because of protected family leave or long-term sickness.

Later amendments can replace this section. Employees should check for an amendment with a more recent effective date before relying on the five-day limit.

## Minimum leave and shutdown days

Employees are encouraged to take at least 20 days each year. Northstar may designate up to two company shutdown days per year. A shutdown day is deducted from annual leave and will be announced at least 60 calendar days in advance.

## Unplanned absence

Annual leave must not be used to conceal sickness or another unplanned absence. Employees who are ill should follow the sickness-reporting process in the Employee Handbook. A manager cannot retrospectively convert sickness absence to annual leave without the employee's agreement.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for monitoring their balance and requesting leave early. Managers are responsible for consistent decisions and adequate coverage. People Operations maintains the official balance and resolves discrepancies.
