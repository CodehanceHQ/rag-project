# Business Travel and Expense Policy

Policy ID: FIN-TRAVEL-022  
Owner: Finance  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 1 March 2026

## General principles

Northstar Analytics reimburses reasonable and necessary costs incurred for approved business travel. Employees should choose the lowest logical cost while considering safety, total travel time, and business needs. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## Approval and booking

All overnight travel requires manager approval before booking. A single booking with an expected cost above EUR 1,200 also requires approval from the relevant department director. Flights and rail journeys must be booked through the approved travel portal unless the Finance Helpdesk grants an exception.

Economy class is required for flights under six scheduled hours. Premium economy may be approved for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a member of the executive team for a documented medical or business-continuity reason.

## Meals and incidental expenses

The standard daily meal allowance is EUR 65 for domestic travel within Spain and EUR 90 for international travel. These are maximum reimbursable amounts, not automatic payments. Meals already included in a hotel rate, conference fee, or customer event must not be claimed again.

City-specific hotel caps and meal exceptions are maintained in `regional-travel-rates.csv`. Where the reference table specifies a different meal allowance, the city-specific value controls.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any individual expense over EUR 25. A card slip showing only the total is not an itemized receipt. If a receipt is unavailable, the employee must provide a written explanation and obtain Finance approval. Repeated missing receipts may cause reimbursement to be declined.

## Ground transportation

Public transport should be used where practical. Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursable for airport transfers, travel before 07:00 or after 22:00, accessibility needs, or locations where public transport is impractical. Car mileage is reimbursed at EUR 0.32 per business kilometre when use of a personal car was approved in advance.

## Submission deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Managers should review them within five working days. Finance may reject claims submitted more than 90 calendar days after the expense unless the Chief Financial Officer approves an exception.

## Lost company property during travel

Loss or theft of a company device is a security incident, not merely an expense matter. The employee must follow the Information Security Policy immediately and must not wait until submitting an expense report.
