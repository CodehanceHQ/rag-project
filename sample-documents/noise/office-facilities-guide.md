# Madrid and Valencia Office Facilities Guide

Document ID: FAC-012  
Owner: Workplace Experience  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 1 May 2026

## Office access

The Madrid office is open from 07:30 to 20:00 on business days. The Valencia collaboration space is open from 08:00 to 19:00. Access outside these hours requires a Workplace Experience request submitted one business day in advance.

## Visitors

Employees must register visitors before arrival and escort them in work areas. Visitor badges must be returned to reception. Visitors may use the guest wireless network but must not connect personal devices to internal wired networks.

## Commuting and bicycle allowance

Employees assigned to Madrid may claim up to EUR 30 per month for secure bicycle parking near the office when the building racks are full. Employees assigned to Valencia may claim a bicycle allowance of up to EUR 45 per month because the collaboration space has no permanent bicycle storage. This is a commuting benefit, not a business-travel expense.

## Meeting rooms

Rooms may be reserved up to 30 days in advance. Reservations should be released when no longer required. The room named Paris seats 12 people; the room named London seats eight. These room names do not indicate travel destinations or reimbursement rates.

## Catering

Team catering requires manager approval. The normal office catering allowance is EUR 18 per attendee. This allowance is separate from the daily meal allowance for approved business travel.

A vague question such as `What is the Valencia allowance?` does not contain enough intent to distinguish the bicycle benefit from travel meal and hotel rates.
