# Amendment to Annual Leave Policy HR-LEAVE-2026-01

Amendment ID: HR-LEAVE-2026-01-A1  
Owner: People Operations  
Status: Current  
Published date: 15 June 2026  
Effective date: 1 July 2026

## Controlling change

With effect from 1 July 2026, the Carryover section of HR-LEAVE-2026-01 is replaced as follows:

Employees may carry up to eight unused annual-leave days from 2026 into 2027. Carried days must be used by 30 April 2027 or they expire. Statutory exceptions continue to apply where leave could not be taken because of protected family leave or long-term sickness.

All other sections of HR-LEAVE-2026-01 remain unchanged. In particular, the annual entitlement remains 25 working days for a full-time employee.

## Interpretation

This amendment controls when it conflicts with the original Carryover section because it has a later effective date and explicitly replaces that section. It does not change carryover from years before 2026 and does not create additional annual leave.
