## Historical annual entitlement

Under this superseded policy, a full-time employee received 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, excluding public holidays. Part-time and partial-year entitlement was calculated pro rata.

## Historical carryover rule

Employees could carry up to three unused days into the next calendar year. Carried leave expired on 28 February unless a statutory exception applied.

## Historical request notice

Requests for five or more consecutive working days had to be submitted at least 10 calendar days in advance. Managers considered business coverage and the order in which requests were received.
